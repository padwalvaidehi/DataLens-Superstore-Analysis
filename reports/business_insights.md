# DataLens: Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
Analyzed retail sales data using Python (Pandas), SQL, Power BI, and Excel to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, and business opportunities.

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Sales:** $2,297,200.86
- **Total Profit:** $286,397.02
- **Units Sold:** 37,873
- **Unique Orders:** 5,009
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 12.47%

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Technology Leads in Profitability
Technology generated $145,454.95 in profit, the highest among the three product categories. Its profit margin was approximately 17.40%.

### 2. Furniture Has a Low Profit Margin
Furniture generated $741,999.80 in sales but only $18,451.27 in profit, giving it a profit margin of approximately 2.49%. This category deserves further investigation.

### 3. Tables Are a Loss-Making Sub-Category
Tables recorded a total loss of approximately $17,725.48. Reviewing pricing, discounting, and costs could help identify ways to improve profitability.

### 4. Higher Discounts Are Associated with Lower Profit Margins
The dataset shows negative profit margins at several higher discount levels. This association suggests that discount policies should be reviewed; it does not by itself prove that discounts cause every loss.

### 5. The West Region Leads in Sales and Profit
The West region generated approximately $725,457.82 in sales and $108,418.45 in profit, the highest totals among the four regions.

### 6. Sales Grew Over the Four-Year Period
Annual sales increased from approximately $484,247 in 2014 to $733,215 in 2017. November 2017 was the highest-sales month in the dataset.

## Tools Used
- **Python:** Data inspection, validation, cleaning, aggregation, and visualization
- **SQL (SQLite):** Business queries and grouped summaries
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and regional filtering
- **Excel:** PivotTables, PivotCharts, and business summaries

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights strong performance in Technology and the West region, alongside profitability concerns in Furniture and Tables. These findings can help guide further investigation into pricing, discounts, and product performance.
